NLS RATING SYSTEM

AL-0 → AL-7 Scale

The AL scale is an NLS impact/severity rating for both avalanches and landslides. Ratings should consider size, speed, runout, structures/roads hit, injuries/fatalities, population exposure, displacement, rescue impact, infrastructure damage and source confidence.

Rating rule: Only the authenticated NLS owner account can publish or change an AL rating. Visitors have public read-only access.
